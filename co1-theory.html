CO1 / COI1 / LECTURE 1 · BI & DECISIONS

What is BI?

• Data: raw facts — a till record

• Information: data with context — sales up 22%

• Insight: the reason — match days

• Action: the decision — stock more snacks

​

In practice

Walmart stocks ahead of storms · Netflix picks each member’s first title · banks spot missed EMIs early · Swiggy and Zomato place riders ahead of demand

Four purposes: visibility, control, learning, foresight — value appears only when a decision changes

Four dashboard types

​

Strategic

CEO and board · quarterly · 5–10 KPIs

​

Tactical

Department heads · weekly · drill-down

​

Operational

Supervisors · real time · alerts

​

Analytical

Analysts · on demand · explore

Avoid: one dashboard for everyone · too many KPIs · numbers without targets · real-time data for a monthly decision

Quality is a gate, not a step

• Data-quality dimensions — check every source, not just one

• Run the three checks: scan-table columns · staff and shifts · arrivals vs scans

• Do the numbers add up? Run a check before you trust a total

• Clean and integrate before you model — a fit-for-purpose dataset

CO1 / COI3 / LECTURE 3 · STAKEHOLDERS

People before charts

Who is in the room

Sponsor · business owner · end users · data stewards · IT · finance · compliance

Power–interest grid: manage sponsors and owners closely; keep finance satisfied; keep users informed and consulted

Methods: interviews, workshops, shadowing, document review, data profiling, prototypes

​

MoSCoW, the labs’ mess rules

Must — a lunch-time decision and the data exist

Should — a decision after lunch and the data exist

Could — no decision named yet

Won’t — the data do not exist

CO1 / COI3 / LECTURE 3 · PREPARATION

Fitting the data for purpose

• Profile: the shape, range and gaps of each source

• Clean: duplicate loads, wrong joins, broken identity

• Integrate and model: dimensional modelling — one fit-for-purpose BI dataset

• Quality is a job at every lifecycle step, not a step itself
