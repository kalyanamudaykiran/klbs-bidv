Start from the decision, not the data

​

The request

Marketing head: “Give me a dashboard on social media.”

The decision behind it: by 30 Nov, split ₹20 L of ad budget across channels.

Work backwards from the decision — not from the data.

​

Decision

30 Nov — split the ₹20 L ad budget across channels

​

Business question

Which channels give the lowest cost per qualified lead?

​

Metric

Cost per qualified lead, in ₹, monthly

Data requirement: ad-platform spend · CRM qualified leads

Visual: cost per lead by channel, against target

Refresh monthly, against the target

CO1 / COI3 / LECTURE 3 · FRAMING

What a complete KPI needs

​

A KPI is complete when it has

name · formula · unit · time grain · source · owner · target

Check it with SMART-D before you build the visual.

​

SMART-D test

Specific · one number, one meaning

Measurable · a formula, a unit

Actionable · someone can act on it

Relevant · tied to a decision

Time-bound · a grain and a target date

Data-available · the source exists

Vanity metric: looks good but drives no decision — page views, followers

Deciding with the pattern

​

₹4,900

Gut feeling — 30 cakes every day

​

₹6,840

Data-informed — bake the averages (+₹1,940 a week)

Perfect hindsight — the best possible week
