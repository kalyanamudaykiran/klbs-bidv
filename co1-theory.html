Part 4 · Dashboards
Four dashboard mistakes
Avoid
One dashboard for everyone
Instead
Build one view for each audience and rhythm.
Avoid
Too many KPIs
Instead
Keep a strategic view to 5–10 KPIs.
Avoid
Numbers without targets
Instead
Show each number against a target or plan.
Avoid
Real-time data for monthly decisions
Instead
Refresh at the pace of the decision.
Choose the dashboard type first, then the KPIs, then the visuals.

Part 5 · Framing questions
Start from the decision, not the data
A marketing head asks for “a dashboard on social media”. Work backwards.
Decision
Decide by 30 November how to split ₹20 lakh of ad budget across channels.
Business question
Which channels give the lowest cost per qualified lead?
Metric
Cost per qualified lead, in ₹, monthly.
Data requirement
Spend from the ad platform; qualified leads from the CRM.
Visual
Cost per qualified lead by channel, against target.

Part 6 · Stakeholders
Prioritise with MoSCoW
Must
The solution fails without it.
Margin must reconcile to the ledger within 0.1%, or the CFO will not trust it.
Should
Important, but a workaround exists.
Could
Valuable but optional.
Alert when a store falls 20% below forecast; nice to have this release.
Won’t
Agreed to leave out of this release.
Before ranking a request such as “add a map”, ask
What would you do differently with this information?
